STEPPER requires macOS 12 or later and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The app is not notarized by Apple and must be approved once under System Settings > Privacy & Security on first launch. The devices shown at the top of this page are 3D renderings of the software interface. STEPPER is software, not hardware. Audio Unit and VST3 plugins are not included. The iPhone controller requires a Mac running STEPPER, connected over Wi-Fi or USB-C. This website plays no audio.
